# Evolutions: forms, Gen 7-9, substitutes

How W2U evolves a Pokémon by its form, where the evolution data for Gen 7-9 and the regional forms comes from, and
which evolutions use a Gen 5 stand-in for a method or item White 2 does not have (2026-10-15).

## Evolution by form

Retail reads a Pokémon's evolutions by species only (`CheckEvolveSpecies`, decomp `src/system/shinka_check.c`) and
keeps the form number when the species changes (`setChangedPkmSpecies`, `src/system/poke_tool.c`). A regional form
therefore evolved as its base species did, into whatever form had its number: Galarian Slowpoke (form 1) into Mega
Slowbro (Slowbro's form 1).

`src/pokedex_expansion/w2u_evolution.cpp` and `Expansion_LimitAdjust.s`:

- `CheckEvolveSpecies` stays retail's (with W2U's patches there for 8 records per member). Its call reading the
  evolution record (offset 0xBA, `getEvolutionInfoFile(species, dest)`) goes to `W2U_Evolution_Read` with the Pokémon
  (kept in r7), which reads the record of its species and form: the evolution archive (`a/0/1/9`) has a member per
  personal record, forms included (`PML_PersonalGetDatID`). A form record with no evolutions uses its species' record,
  as retail always did: cosmetic forms (Floette's colours, Roaming Gimmighoul, Pikachu's forms) evolve as their
  species does.
- `setChangedPkmSpecies` (the evolution demo, for the Pokémon and the copy it shows) gives the new species a form when
  the species changes: the one `data/pml/evolution_forms.bin` names for (species, form, new species), else the same
  form number if the new species has it, else form 0. The table: Galarian Slowpoke into Slowbro form 2, Galarian
  Darumaka into Darmanitan form 2, Pikachu's forms 1-6 into Raichu form 0 (Raichu's forms 1-3 are its Megas and
  Alolan Raichu). Format: 8-byte entries `{u16 species, u16 new species, u8 form, u8 new form, u16 0}` until species 0,
  the file 512 bytes (64 entries; `W2U_PATH_EVOLUTION_FORMS`, staged like `poke_form_list.bin`, in Black 2's package
  too).
- One record can name only a species, so a Pokémon has one evolution per target species: Dunsparce into Two-Segment
  Dudunsparce, Toxel into Amped Toxtricity, Lechonk into Oinkologne (form 0), Kubfu into Single Strike Urshifu. The
  Hisuian forms that evolve from their region (Hisuian Typhlosion from Quilava in Hisui) are not reached by evolution.

Checked in game (`w2u-local/harness/evo_forms.yml`): Galarian Slowpoke 36 -> 37 becomes Slowbro form 2 (traced: form
set to 2 before the species), Galarian Meowth becomes Perrserker, Alolan Rattata becomes Alolan Raticate, Rowlet
evolves at 17; Froakie's evolution (`wave_dbg.yml` EVO_FROAKIE) is unchanged.

## Evolution method names

`tools/mkdata/enum/evolution_methods.toml` and `data/enums/evolution_methods.yml` named methods 23-28 one place off
(23 is "level up, male", decomp `EVO_METHOD_LEVEL_MALE`; Pokeweb's list agrees) and 29-32 after methods the game does
not have; the data kept retail's numbers. They now read as the game does: 23 `METHOD_LEVELUP_MALE`, 24
`METHOD_LEVELUP_FEMALE`, 25 `METHOD_LOCATION_ELEC` (Chargestone Cave), 26 `METHOD_LOCATION_MOSS` (Pinwheel Forest),
27 `METHOD_LOCATION_ICE` (Twist Mountain), 28 `METHOD_LOCATION_ELEC2` (unused), 29-31 the battle-log companion's
KO / battle counts (docs/battle-log-save-format.md). Eevee's two location evolutions had been swapped by the old
names (Moss Rock gave Glaceon): back to retail's.

## Data: Gen 7-9 and regional forms

Generated by `w2u-local/formdata/gen_evolutions.py` from Showdown's `data/pokedex.ts` (prevo, evoType, evoLevel,
evoItem, evoMove, evoCondition): 147 evolutions in 142 records.

- Gen 7-9 species (no evolution data before), older species evolving into Gen 7-9 ones (Primeape into Annihilape,
  Scyther into Kleavor, ...), added in free records; their existing evolutions stay.
- Regional form records (they copied their base species'): the form's own evolutions only.
- Region-bound evolutions (Pikachu into Alolan Raichu in Alola) are left out: they would replace the normal ones.
